# OK-Omni-Known-A-Linguistic-Spiritual-Interpretation-of-Universal-Awareness
Reinterpreting “OK” as Omni Known — a fusion of linguistics, philosophy, and consciousness studies linking Latin omnis and Sanskrit jñāna through universal awareness. Developed under Sri Yoga Center Ashram Trust – Kunarpur, part of the “Global Health Innovation and Integration” initiative.

## 🕉️ OK = Omni Known — Linguistic & Philosophical Origin

> **Coined by:** Dr. Rajatsubhra Mukhopadhyay (2025)  
> **Meaning:** *Universal Awareness* — “All is Known.”

---

### 🌐 Linguistic Breakdown

| Component | Language | Root | Meaning | Relation |
|------------|-----------|-------|-----------|-----------|
| **Omni-** | Latin | *omnis* | all, universal, complete | Source of “omnipresent,” “omniscient” |
| **Know** | Old English | *cnāwan* | to perceive, understand | From Proto-Germanic *knēaną* |
| *(Know → PIE Root)* | Proto-Indo-European | ***ǵneh₃-*** | to know, recognize, be aware | Common ancestor for *gnosis*, *jñāna* |

---

### 🌿 Related Cognates

| Family | Word | Meaning | Language |
|---------|------|----------|-----------|
| Sanskrit | **jñāna (ज्ञान)** | inner knowledge, realization | Indo-Aryan |
| Greek | **gnosis (γνῶσις)** | spiritual insight | Hellenic |
| Latin | **cognitio / agnoscere** | recognition, awareness | Italic |
| English | **know / knowledge / acknowledge** | awareness, understanding | Germanic |

---

### 🔆 Philosophical Interpretation

**“Omni Known”** combines:  
- **Latin** universality (*Omni = All*)  
- **Indo-European / Sanskrit** awareness (*Know ↔ Jñāna ↔ Gnosis*)  

Together, they express the state where  
> **The Soul recognizes all as One Consciousness —  
> the Universe already Known within Itself.**
